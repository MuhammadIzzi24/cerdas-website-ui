How access works

The same three steps whether you are an internal theme, another UTP centre or an industry partner.

ALLOCATION REVIEW
EACH SEMESTER
01
Scope the requirement
Tell us the workload, not the machine you think you need. We will say which platform fits and whether the queue can absorb it.
02
Sign the access agreement
One document covering allocation, data retention, publication rights and who is named on any resulting IP.
03
Book and run
Allocated platforms run on a semester grant of hours; bookable ones open in half-day blocks. Usage is reported back to you.
Charging
Internal use is covered by the centre allocation. External use is charged at the published rate card, or waived where the work forms part of a joint grant with CERDAS named as a partner.
