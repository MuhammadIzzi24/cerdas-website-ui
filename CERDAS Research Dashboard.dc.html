How the numbers move

Five stages tie every figure above back to a decision. Nothing on this page is collected for its own sake.

VALUE CHAIN
QUARTERLY CYCLE
01
Generate & Deliver
GROUP HEADS & PROJECT TEAMS
The six research groups produce the output — papers, graduates, prototypes, project milestones. This is where the targets are actually met or missed.
02
Enable & Accelerate
FUNCTIONAL ARMS
Grants, innovation, industry, international and facilities offices clear the path — proposals, agreements, filings, compute allocation.
03
Monitor & Report
STRATEGY, PLANNING & REPORTING
Each KPI line is collected quarterly against a named evidence file. Unevidenced lines are reported as unevidenced, not as zero.
04
Review & Decide
DIRECTOR & MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE
Quarterly review moves effort, budget and facility time towards the areas that are slipping. Below-target areas carry a written action.
05
Deliver Impact
CENTRE, WITH PARTNERS
Adopted technology, trained talent and published evidence go back to industry, to the university, and into the HiCoE return.

Who is accountable

Three lines of accountability, and they do not overlap.

OWNS PERFORMANCE
Group Heads own research performance
Publications, postgraduate completions, project delivery and translation readiness sit with the six Group Heads. Where an area on this page is below target, a Group Head carries the action — not the office that reported it.
ENABLES AND MONITORS
Functional arms enable and monitor
The grants, innovation, industry, international, talent and facilities offices open doors, run the process and collect the evidence. They do not hold the targets, and they do not set them.
ACCOUNTABLE OVERALL
The Director owns overall performance
The Director is accountable to the university and to HiCoE for the centre's position as a whole, including the balance between areas and the decision to publish these figures.
METHOD
Figures are compiled from Scopus, UTP RIMS and the centre's quarterly returns, and are stated against the target set for that period. Current-period figures are provisional until the quarterly review signs them off. Where a KPI line has no evidence file, it is counted as unevidenced.
PUBLISHING CADENCE
Refreshed quarterly, within two weeks of the management review. Annual HiCoE return filed each January.
DATA QUERIES
Strategy, Planning & Reporting Unit, CERDAS.
