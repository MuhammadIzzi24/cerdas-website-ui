What the framework is for

Four goals. Every appointment type above exists to serve at least one of them.

01
Strengthen research excellence and impact
Tiered researcher roles tie leadership expectations to academic standing, so the people setting direction are the people delivering.
02
Foster collaboration across academia and industry
Visiting and adjunct categories give external researchers and practitioners a real, named place inside the centre.
03
Develop and nurture research talent
The pipeline is a route, not a label: school outreach to final-year project to research degree to staff appointment.
04
Create sustainable solutions
Long appointments and long partnerships outlast single grants — which is what it takes for research to reach deployment.
