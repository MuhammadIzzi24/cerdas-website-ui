Two routes in

Both run through the Innovation & Impact Translation arm, and both start with a conversation rather than a form you never hear back from.

ROUTE 01

License our technology

For something already built. You get a licence to a filed or granted invention, the software that implements it, and a support period while your team takes it on.

Exclusive or non-exclusive, by field of use
Fee plus royalty, or a single buy-out for narrow fields
Typical time from first call to signed licence: 3–5 months
NAMED CONTACT
Emelia Akashah Bt Patah Akhir

ROUTE 02

Co-develop with us

For something that does not exist yet. We build it together on your data and your constraints, with ownership and licensing agreed before the work starts.

Contract research, joint grant, or a funded PhD
Background and foreground IP separated in writing at the start
Typical time from first call to project start: 2–4 months
NAMED CONTACT
Yew Kwang Hooi
Functional Lead · Industry & Strategic Partnerships

Start the conversation

Tell us the problem in your own words. You will hear from a named person, not a queue — usually within three working days.

IP POLICY
Intellectual property created at the centre is owned by Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS. Inventors are named and share in licensing income under university policy.
UTP'S ROLE
The university signs the agreement, holds the IP and issues the licence. CERDAS provides the technology, the team and the technical support; the university's legal and finance offices handle the contract.
