How research groups and strategic functions work together

The two halves of the structure do different jobs, and the distinction is deliberate.

RG01–RG06 — DELIVER

Research groups do the research

A research group is where the work happens: proposals written, projects run, papers published, students supervised, prototypes built. Each group has a Group Head who owns its targets and decides how its researchers and facility time are used.

Groups are organised around research questions, not departments — a researcher can sit in more than one, and most flagship projects deliberately span two.

F1–F4 — ENABLE

Strategic functions clear the path

A strategic function holds no research targets of its own. It exists to remove what slows the groups down — finding the funding call, drafting the agreement, filing the patent, booking the cluster, collecting the KPI evidence.

Functions cut across all six research groups. That is the point: one route to industry, one appointment process, one performance return, rather than six of each.

Combining six focused research groups with clear governance and accountable leadership is how CERDAS intends to drive the journey from research excellence to sustainable impact.
