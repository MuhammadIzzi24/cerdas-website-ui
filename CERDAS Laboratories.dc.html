Laboratories

Six named laboratories, each attached to a research group with its own lead, postgraduate cohort and access rules. Partners work inside them, not alongside them.

Working in our labs

Three things to settle before a visiting researcher or partner engineer starts.

TYPICAL LEAD TIME
2–3 WEEKS
01
Safety induction
A half-day induction covering electrical safety, the test-rig lockout procedure and the incident-reporting route. Required once, valid for two years.
02
Data and IP agreement
Any partner dataset entering a lab needs a signed agreement naming the retention period and who may see it. The centre will not start work without one.
03
Named host
Every visitor is hosted by a fellow who is accountable for their access, bench space and supervision for the duration of the visit.
