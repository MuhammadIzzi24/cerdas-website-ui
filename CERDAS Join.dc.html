Why organisations work with a university centre rather than a consultancy

Because the problems worth bringing here are the ones nobody can quote a fixed answer for. We take the questions that need a method invented, not a template applied — and you keep the method afterwards.

DEPTH OVER SPEED
A consultancy is optimised to finish. A research centre is optimised to be right — which is the better trade when the answer will run in your operation for a decade.
THE METHOD IS YOURS
You get the model, the training code and a team that can retrain it — not a black box with a renewal date attached.
TALENT, AS A SIDE EFFECT
Funded students work on your problem for three years and know your domain when they graduate. Several partners have hired the researcher who built their system.
PUBLIC FUNDING LEVERAGE
On a joint grant, your contribution is the industry share of a much larger programme. RM 1 of partner cash has typically unlocked RM 3.20 of national funding.
