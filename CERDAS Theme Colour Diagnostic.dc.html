Can you tell the four research groups apart?

The §7.2 blue ladder assigns each theme one step of a single-hue lightness ramp. This page renders those four values at the sizes the site actually uses them, and measures the separation between every pair.

Verdict: it is failing. Every adjacent pair sits near 1.5:1 — half the 3:1 minimum for a non-text UI distinction — and a four-step single-hue ramp cannot reach 3:1 per step at any lightness. The T-code, not the colour, is doing all the identifying work today.
