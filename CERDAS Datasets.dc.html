Data governance

Resilient data is in the centre's name, so it should be visible in how we handle it. Four commitments apply to every dataset above.

Questions about a specific dataset go to its named contact. Questions about the policy go to the Operations, Resources & Performance arm.

01 · PROVENANCE
Every record carries its source, collection method and processing history. If we cannot say where a value came from, it does not go in the dataset.
02 · PARTNER DATA STAYS PARTNER DATA
Industrial data is released only within the terms of the agreement that produced it. Where a partner cannot open the data, we publish the method and the benchmark instead.
03 · HONEST ABOUT ANONYMISATION
Operational telemetry can often be re-identified. Where that risk exists we say so and use the restricted level rather than claiming the data is anonymous.
04 · RETENTION AND WITHDRAWAL
Datasets are kept for at least ten years after publication. If one is withdrawn, the DOI resolves to a notice explaining why — it is never silently removed.
